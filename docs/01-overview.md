# Overview

**AccountingMzansi** is an interactive companion for **CAPS Accounting, Grades 10, 11 and 12**, with a **Money Life** track that teaches the money skills every young adult needs. It is the sister app of *MathsMzansi*, *CodeMzansi* (IT), *GeoMzansi* (Geography) and *PhysicsMzansi*, and shares their learning design and accessibility features.

## What's inside

- **40 lessons** covering all **30 CAPS topics** and **8 Money Life topics**, each split into 3–10 minute steps.
- **22 hands-on labs**: the accounting-equation balance scale, a T-account ledger, a source-document sorter, a drag-and-drop statement builder, VAT and payslip labs, a SARS tax calculator, a budget builder, compound-interest and credit labs, depreciation, break-even, production-cost, ratio, inventory-valuation, bank-reconciliation, cash-budget and partnership labs, a side-hustle business simulator and a formula sheet.
- **28 question generators** with full worked solutions – practice that never runs out.
- **Typed answers that understand accountants**: `R12 450`, `12 450,50`, `(R5 000)` for negatives, `35,5%`, `1,45 : 1`. Feedback spots VAT-inclusive/exclusive slips, % vs decimal, monthly vs yearly and upside-down ratios.
- **Flashcards** with spaced repetition for 280+ concepts, plus a glossary.
- **Quiz Arena** (165 questions), **Exam Room** (53 NSC-style questions, 155 parts, 428 marks with memos) and **timed mock papers** for Paper 1 and Paper 2 in every grade.
- **Games**: Speed Solve, Debit or Credit Dash, Concept Pairs, True/False Blitz, Odd One Out and Accounting Hangman.
- **Weak-spot coach**, **worksheet generator** and a **progress dashboard** with XP, levels, streaks and badges.

## Money Life

The Money Life track is not examined, but it may be the most useful part of the subject: budgets, bank accounts and scams, starting a side hustle, reading a payslip, credit and interest, saving and investing, **doing your own taxes on SARS eFiling**, tax for side hustlers, and adulting (renting, cars, insurance, retirement). Every lesson links back to the CAPS content it uses.

## Principles

1. **Do, don't just read.** Every idea has a lab, a sort, a match or a question.
2. **Small steps.** One idea per screen, worked examples revealed a step at a time.
3. **Immediate, kind feedback.** "Not yet", hints and full solutions.
4. **Every brain is welcome.** ADHD and dyslexia support is built in, not bolted on.
5. **Private by design.** No accounts, no tracking – progress stays on the device.

> An independent study aid. Always confirm exam details with your teacher and the latest DBE examination guidelines, and tax details with SARS.
