# CAPS curriculum map

Every lesson follows the CAPS Accounting content for Grades 10–12 (Financial Accounting, Managerial Accounting and Managing Resources). Paper 1 is mainly Financial Accounting; Paper 2 is mainly Managerial Accounting and Managing Resources. Mark allocations per paper are approximate – always check the latest DBE examination guidelines.

## Grade 10

| Topic | Knowledge area | Paper | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounting Concepts & GAAP | Bookkeeping | 1 | What accounting is, who uses it, the forms of ownership, and the GAAP principles that keep everyone honest. |
| The Accounting Equation | Bookkeeping | 1 | Assets = Owner's equity + Liabilities, and how every transaction keeps the scale balanced. |
| Source Documents & Journals | Bookkeeping | 1 | Receipts, invoices, cheques and credit notes – and the CRJ, CPJ, DJ, CJ, DAJ, CAJ, petty cash and General Journal. |
| General Ledger & Trial Balance | Bookkeeping | 1 | Double entry, posting to T-accounts, balancing accounts, the debtors' and creditors' ledgers, and the Trial Balance. |
| Financial Statements of a Sole Trader | Financial Reporting | 1 | Closing transfers, the Income Statement, the Balance Sheet and the first year-end adjustments. |
| Salaries & Wages | Tax, VAT & Payroll | 2 | Gross and net pay, deductions and contributions, the salaries journal and the wages journal. |
| Value-Added Tax (VAT) | Tax, VAT & Payroll | 2 | Input and output VAT, VAT-inclusive and exclusive prices, zero-rated and exempt items, and VAT payable to SARS. |
| Bank Reconciliation | Managing Resources | 2 | Comparing the cash journals with the bank statement: outstanding items, errors and the reconciliation statement. |
| Ethics & Internal Control | Managing Resources | 2 | Ethical behaviour, the professional bodies, and internal controls that protect cash and stock. |
| Cost Accounting Basics | Managerial Accounting | 2 | Fixed and variable costs, direct and indirect costs, cost price, mark-up and selling price. |
| Budgeting Basics | Managerial Accounting | 2 | Personal and business budgets, budgeted vs actual figures, and why budgets matter. |
| Inventory Systems | Managing Resources | 2 | The perpetual and periodic inventory systems, cost of sales and stock control. |

## Grade 11

| Topic | Knowledge area | Paper | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year-end Adjustments & Final Accounts | Financial Reporting | 1 | Accruals, prepayments, depreciation, bad debts, provision for bad debts, inventory deficits – and the financial statements. |
| Partnerships | Financial Reporting | 1 | Capital and current accounts, the appropriation account, partners' salaries, interest on capital and the profit-sharing ratio. |
| Clubs (Non-profit Organisations) | Financial Reporting | 1 | Membership fees, the Receipts and Payments Account, the Income and Expenditure Statement and accumulated funds. |
| Fixed Assets & Depreciation | Managing Resources | 1 | Cost price, the straight-line and diminishing-balance methods, the asset disposal account and the fixed asset note. |
| Analysis & Interpretation | Financial Reporting | 1 | Profitability, liquidity, solvency and return ratios – and what they tell the owner. |
| Debtors & Creditors Reconciliations | Managing Resources | 2 | Reconciling the debtors' list with the control account, and a creditor's statement with the creditors' ledger. |
| Manufacturing Cost Accounting | Managerial Accounting | 2 | Direct materials, direct labour, factory overheads, the Production Cost Statement and cost per unit. |
| Cash Budgets | Managerial Accounting | 2 | Projected receipts and payments, debtors' collection schedules and comments on a budget. |

## Grade 12

| Topic | Knowledge area | Paper | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Companies: Concepts & Ledger | Bookkeeping | 1 | Companies Act, shares and shareholders, ordinary share capital, share buy-backs, retained income, income tax and dividends. |
| Company Financial Statements | Financial Reporting | 1 | The Income Statement, Balance Sheet and notes of a company, including adjustments and the audit report. |

| Reconciliations | Managing Resources | 2 | Bank, debtors and creditors reconciliations at matric level, with internal-control comments. |
| VAT at Matric Level | Tax, VAT & Payroll | 2 | VAT calculations, the VAT control account, bad debts and drawings, and ethical problems with VAT. |
| Cost Accounting & Break-even | Managerial Accounting | 2 | Production cost statements, unit costs, fixed and variable costs, the break-even point and problem-solving. |
| Budgeting & Projected Statements | Managerial Accounting | 2 | Cash budgets, projected income statements, variances and internal control over budgets. |
| Inventory Valuation | Managing Resources | 2 | FIFO, weighted average and specific identification, stock-holding period and inventory turnover. |

## How the papers are built

| Grade | Paper 1 – Financial Accounting | Paper 2 – Managerial Accounting & Managing Resources |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Concepts, equation, journals, ledger, Trial Balance, statements | Salaries, VAT, bank reconciliation, ethics & control, cost, budgets, inventory |
| 11 | Adjustments, sole traders, partnerships, clubs, fixed assets, analysis | Reconciliations, manufacturing cost accounting, cash budgets |
| 12 | Companies, statements, cash flow, analysis, audit & governance | Reconciliations, VAT, cost & break-even, budgets, inventory valuation |

Each paper is 150 marks in 2 hours (about 0,8 minutes per mark).
