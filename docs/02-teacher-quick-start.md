# Teacher quick-start (10 minutes)

## 1. Find your topic

Open **Learn** and filter by grade and knowledge area – or use the **CAPS curriculum map** in these docs. Each lesson shows its CAPS reference, objectives and how long it takes.

## 2. Teach with the labs

Project a lab from **Labs** (the Toolkit) on the board:

| Teaching… | Use |
|---|---|
| The accounting equation | Accounting equation balance – learners shout +, − or 0 before you post |
| Double entry | T-account ledger lab – then draw up the Trial Balance together |
| Source documents | Which journal? – real-looking receipts, invoices and credit notes |
| Financial statements | Statement builder – drag accounts into sections; it checks that it balances |
| Partnerships | Partnership appropriation – change the agreement live |
| Reconciliations | Bank reconciliation lab – tick, update, reconcile |
| Cost accounting | Production cost statement and break-even lab |
| Budgets | Cash budget lab – watch the overdraft appear |
| VAT and payroll | VAT lab and payslip builder |

## 3. Set practice

- **Practice blocks** in every lesson are checked instantly.
- **Endless drills** generate fresh questions for 28 skills.
- **Worksheet generator** (Teachers → Worksheets): choose topics and a number of questions, print the questions and a full memo. The link reproduces the same sheet.

## 4. Prepare for exams

- **Exam Room**: NSC-style questions with memos. Learners mark themselves, tick by tick.
- **Mock papers**: full or quick (~50 marks), timed, untimed or with 25% extra time. Learners get an NSC level and a breakdown by topic.
- **Weak-spot coach** on the Progress page tells each learner what to revise next.

## 5. Use Money Life in Life Orientation or EMS

The Money Life lessons work as stand-alone financial-literacy lessons: budgets, banking and scams, payslips, credit, investing and filing a tax return. They suit Grade 10–12 Accounting, LO or EMS bridging lessons.

## 6. Accessibility in one click

Show learners the **Preferences** button (top right): dyslexia-friendly fonts, larger text, reading ruler, word anchors, step mode, calm mode, focus sprints and read-aloud.
