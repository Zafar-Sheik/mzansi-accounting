# FAQ & troubleshooting

**My answer is marked wrong but I'm sure it's right.**

- Check the period: monthly or yearly?
- VAT: should the amount include or exclude VAT?
- Percentages: type `35,5` or `35,5%`, not `0,355`.
- A loss or decrease: use a minus sign or brackets, e.g. `(R1 400)`.
- Round only at the end. Rand answers must be right to the cent.

**Which tax tables are used?** The SARS 2025/26 tables (1 March 2025 – 28 February 2026). Tax tables change in every Budget Speech – learners should always check www.sars.gov.za before filing a real return.

**Is Money Life examined?** No. It is a financial-literacy track that links CAPS skills to real life. It is never included in mock papers.

**Is Grade 10 included?** Yes – all three FET grades, 30 CAPS topics across Paper 1 and Paper 2.

**Does it replace the textbook?** No. It is a study aid with practice, labs and exam questions. Learners still need to practise full formats (journals, ledgers, statements) on paper, as in the exam.

**Animations make me dizzy.** Turn on *Calm mode* in Preferences, or your device's "reduce motion" setting.

**Where is my progress?** On this device only (in your browser). Use *Export* on the Progress page to move it to another device.
