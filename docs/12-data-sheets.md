# Formula sheet, ratios & SARS tables

The same reference is available inside the app as the **Formula & reference sheet** lab (Labs → Formula sheet).

## Ratios and formulas

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Gross profit on sales | Gross profit ÷ Sales × 100 |
| Gross profit on cost of sales (mark-up) | Gross profit ÷ Cost of sales × 100 |
| Net profit on sales | Net profit ÷ Sales × 100 |
| Operating expenses on sales | Operating expenses ÷ Sales × 100 |
| Current ratio | Current assets : Current liabilities |
| Acid-test ratio | (Current assets − Inventories) : Current liabilities |
| Stock-holding period | Average inventories ÷ Cost of sales × 365 |
| Stock turnover rate | Cost of sales ÷ Average inventories |
| Debtors collection period | Average debtors ÷ Credit sales × 365 |
| Creditors payment period | Average creditors ÷ Credit purchases × 365 |
| Debt-equity ratio | Non-current liabilities : Equity |
| Solvency ratio | Total assets : Total liabilities |
| Return on equity (ROSHE) | Net profit after tax ÷ Average equity × 100 |
| Return on capital employed (ROTCE) | (Net profit before tax + interest on loans) ÷ Average (equity + non-current liabilities) × 100 |
| Earnings per share | Net profit after tax ÷ Weighted number of shares × 100 (cents) |
| Dividends per share | Total dividends ÷ Number of shares × 100 (cents) |
| Dividend pay-out rate | DPS ÷ EPS × 100 |
| Net asset value per share | Shareholders' equity ÷ Number of shares × 100 (cents) |
| Break-even point | Fixed costs ÷ (Selling price per unit − Variable cost per unit) |
| Average | (Opening + Closing) ÷ 2 |

## VAT and payroll

- VAT 15%. Exclusive × 115/100 = inclusive. Inclusive × 15/115 = VAT.
- VAT payable = output VAT − input VAT.
- UIF: 1% employee + 1% employer, on earnings up to R17 712 a month (max R177,12 each).
- SDL: 1% of payroll (employer only).
- Company income tax 27%; dividends tax 20%.

## SARS individual tax tables – 2025/26

| Taxable income | Rate of tax |
|---|---|
| R1 – R237 100 | 18% of taxable income |
| R237 101 – R370 500 | R42 678 + 26% of taxable income above R237 100 |
| R370 501 – R512 800 | R77 362 + 31% above R370 500 |
| R512 801 – R673 000 | R121 475 + 36% above R512 800 |
| R673 001 – R857 900 | R179 147 + 39% above R673 000 |
| R857 901 – R1 817 000 | R251 258 + 41% above R857 900 |
| R1 817 001 and above | R644 489 + 45% above R1 817 000 |

Rebates: primary R17 235; secondary (65+) R9 444; tertiary (75+) R3 145. Thresholds: under 65 R95 750; 65–74 R148 217; 75+ R165 689. Medical tax credits: R364 a month for the main member and first dependant, R246 for each additional dependant. Retirement contributions: deductible up to 27,5% of income, maximum R350 000 a year. Tax-free savings: R36 000 a year, R500 000 lifetime.
