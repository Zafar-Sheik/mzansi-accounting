# Money Life: the financial-literacy track

Money Life teaches the money skills young South Africans need – using the accounting they learn for the exam. It is **not examined** and never appears in mock papers, but every lesson links to a CAPS topic.

## Lessons

| Lesson | Hands-on tools | CAPS link |
|---|---|---|
| Your money plan | Needs/wants sorter, budget builder (50/30/20), budget steps | Gr 10 budgeting |
| Banking, cards & scams | Scam-or-legit sorter, bank-fee maths | Gr 10 bank reconciliation, internal control |
| Start a side hustle | Side-hustle simulator, pricing drills, invoice checklist | Gr 10 equation, journals, cost & mark-up |
| Your first job & payslip | Payslip builder (real PAYE and UIF), BCEA basics | Gr 10 salaries & wages |
| Credit, debt & interest | Loan lab, minimum-payment trap, credit-score sorter | Liabilities, interest, debt-equity ratio |
| Saving & investing | Compound-interest lab (start now vs later), inflation drills | Interest, shares, returns |
| How to do your own taxes | SARS tax calculator, eFiling sequence, documents table | Gr 10 PAYE, Gr 12 tax |
| Tax for side hustlers | Deductible-expense sorter, provisional & turnover tax | VAT, company tax, business entity |
| Adulting 101 | Car depreciation lab, insurance match, two-pot retirement | Fixed assets, depreciation, budgets |

## The Money Life hub

`/money-life` collects the lessons, the six most useful calculators (budget, tax, payslip, side hustle, compound interest, credit) and a private "money checklist" (nothing is saved or sent).

## Teaching tips

- Use the **side-hustle simulator** as a lesson starter: learners compare profits, then explain why their cash differs from their profit.
- Use the **payslip builder** before teaching the Salaries Journal – learners see why PAYE and UIF exist.
- In tax season (July–October), walk through **How to do your own taxes** with the eFiling sequence and tax calculator.
- Stress the safety callouts: never share OTPs, check FSCA registration, beware of "guaranteed" returns.

## Keeping it current

Tax, UIF, TFSA and retirement figures change. They all live in `src/lib/acc/tax.ts`; lesson text mentions the tax year (2025/26) and always tells learners to check www.sars.gov.za.
